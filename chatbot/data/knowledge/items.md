# Finding items

## Where to buy basic items
Poké Marts (or the shop counter in Pokémon Centers in newer games) sell Poké Balls, Potions, status healers and Repels. Their stock grows as you earn more gym badges: Great Balls, Super Potions, Ultra Balls, Hyper Potions, Max Potions, Full Restores and Revives become available later. Department stores and special shops in big cities sell more, such as vitamins and battle items.

## Where to find evolution stones
Evolution stones such as Fire, Water, Thunder, Leaf, Moon, Sun, Shiny, Dusk, Dawn and Ice Stones are found in different places in each game. In the Kanto games many stones are sold at the Celadon Department Store, and Moon Stones are found in Mt. Moon. In newer games stones are often picked up as overworld items, bought in certain shops, or earned as rewards from raids and side activities. Check a guide for the specific game you are playing.

## Rare Candy
Rare Candies are hidden or visible items in the overworld in every game. In Sword and Shield they are common Max Raid Battle rewards, and in Scarlet and Violet they are common Tera Raid Battle rewards. They can't be bought in most games, though some offer them through special shops or points.

## Master Ball
In most games you receive one Master Ball from the story, often from the villain team's boss or the professor. Extra Master Balls are rare: some games give them through lotteries or special events, and in Scarlet and Violet they can be rare rewards from high-level Tera Raid Battles.

## TMs (Technical Machines)
TMs teach moves. Older games had single-use TMs and reusable HMs; since Generation 5 TMs are reusable. In Sword and Shield TMs are found and bought, and TRs are single-use. In Scarlet and Violet most TMs are crafted at TM machines in Pokémon Centers using League Points and materials gathered from defeated Pokémon, after first finding or unlocking the recipe.

## Battle items like Leftovers and Choice items
Competitive held items such as Leftovers, Choice Band, Choice Scarf, Life Orb, Focus Sash and Assault Vest are usually found as rare overworld items or bought with Battle Points at the Battle Tower or Battle Frontier. In Scarlet and Violet many of these items are sold at Delibird Presents shops after earning enough badges.

## Ability Capsules, Ability Patches and Bottle Caps
Ability Capsules and Ability Patches change a Pokémon's ability, and Bottle Caps power up IVs through Hyper Training. They are usually exchanged for Battle Points, found as rare items, or earned from raids (Max Raids in Sword and Shield, Tera Raids in Scarlet and Violet).
