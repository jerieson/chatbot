# Regions

## Kanto
Kanto is the first region, featured in Pokémon Red, Blue and Yellow, the remakes FireRed and LeafGreen, and Let's Go Pikachu and Let's Go Eevee. The professor is Professor Oak and the villains are Team Rocket, led by Giovanni. Gym leaders: Brock (Rock, Pewter City), Misty (Water, Cerulean City), Lt. Surge (Electric, Vermilion City), Erika (Grass, Celadon City), Koga (Poison, Fuchsia City), Sabrina (Psychic, Saffron City), Blaine (Fire, Cinnabar Island) and Giovanni (Ground, Viridian City). The Elite Four are Lorelei, Bruno, Agatha and Lance, and the champion is your rival. Kanto can also be visited after the main story of Gold, Silver, Crystal, HeartGold and SoulSilver.

## Johto
Johto is the region of Pokémon Gold, Silver and Crystal and the remakes HeartGold and SoulSilver. The professor is Professor Elm, and Team Rocket returns as the villain team. Gym leaders: Falkner (Flying, Violet City), Bugsy (Bug, Azalea Town), Whitney (Normal, Goldenrod City), Morty (Ghost, Ecruteak City), Chuck (Fighting, Cianwood City), Jasmine (Steel, Olivine City), Pryce (Ice, Mahogany Town) and Clair (Dragon, Blackthorn City). The Elite Four are Will, Koga, Bruno and Karen, and the champion is Lance.

## Hoenn
Hoenn is the region of Pokémon Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald and the remakes Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire. The professor is Professor Birch, and the villains are Team Magma and Team Aqua. Gym leaders: Roxanne (Rock), Brawly (Fighting), Wattson (Electric), Flannery (Fire), Norman (Normal), Winona (Flying), Tate and Liza (Psychic) and Wallace (Water; Juan in Emerald). The Elite Four are Sidney, Phoebe, Glacia and Drake, and the champion is Steven (Wallace in Emerald).

## Sinnoh
Sinnoh is the region of Pokémon Diamond, Pearl and Platinum and the remakes Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl. Pokémon Legends: Arceus takes place in its past, when it was called Hisui. The professor is Professor Rowan and the villains are Team Galactic, led by Cyrus. Gym leaders include Roark (Rock), Gardenia (Grass), Maylene (Fighting), Crasher Wake (Water), Fantina (Ghost), Byron (Steel), Candice (Ice) and Volkner (Electric). The Elite Four are Aaron, Bertha, Flint and Lucian, and the champion is Cynthia.

## Unova
Unova is the region of Pokémon Black and White and Black 2 and White 2. The professor is Professor Juniper and the villains are Team Plasma, with N and Ghetsis. Gym leaders in Black and White include Cilan, Chili and Cress, Lenora (Normal), Burgh (Bug), Elesa (Electric), Clay (Ground), Skyla (Flying), Brycen (Ice) and Drayden or Iris (Dragon). The Elite Four are Shauntal, Marshal, Grimsley and Caitlin. The champion is Alder in Black and White and Iris in Black 2 and White 2.

## Kalos
Kalos is the region of Pokémon X and Y, inspired by France, and introduced Mega Evolution and the Fairy type. The professor is Professor Sycamore and the villains are Team Flare, led by Lysandre. Gym leaders: Viola (Bug), Grant (Rock), Korrina (Fighting), Ramos (Grass), Clemont (Electric), Valerie (Fairy), Olympia (Psychic) and Wulfric (Ice). The Elite Four are Malva, Siebold, Wikstrom and Drasna, and the champion is Diantha. Pokémon Legends: Z-A takes place in Lumiose City.

## Alola
Alola is the region of Pokémon Sun and Moon and Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon, inspired by Hawaii. Instead of gyms, trainers take the island challenge, completing trials led by captains and battling each island's kahuna. The professor is Professor Kukui, and the story involves Team Skull and the Aether Foundation, led by Lusamine. Alola introduced Z-Moves and Alolan forms, and the player becomes Alola's first champion.

## Galar
Galar is the region of Pokémon Sword and Shield, inspired by Great Britain, with the Isle of Armor and Crown Tundra expansions. The professor is Professor Magnolia, helped by her granddaughter Sonia. Gym battles are big stadium events, and gym leaders include Milo (Grass), Nessa (Water), Kabu (Fire), Bea or Allister (Fighting or Ghost), Opal (Fairy), Gordie or Melony (Rock or Ice), Piers (Dark) and Raihan (Dragon). The champion is Leon. Galar introduced Dynamax, Gigantamax and the open Wild Area.

## Paldea
Paldea is the region of Pokémon Scarlet and Violet, inspired by Spain and Portugal, with The Teal Mask and The Indigo Disk expansions. It is an open world with three story paths: Victory Road (the gyms), Path of Legends (giant Titan Pokémon) and Starfall Street (Team Star). The professor is Professor Sada in Scarlet and Professor Turo in Violet. Gym leaders: Katy (Bug), Brassius (Grass), Iono (Electric), Kofu (Water), Larry (Normal), Ryme (Ghost), Tulip (Psychic) and Grusha (Ice). The Elite Four are Rika, Poppy, Larry and Hassel, the Top Champion is Geeta, and your rival Nemona is already a champion. Paldea introduced Terastallization.
